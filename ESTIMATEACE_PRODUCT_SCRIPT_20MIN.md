## FULL NARRATION SCRIPT

### 0) Cold open — What is EstimateAce? (≈1 min)

Hi — this is EstimateAce: the all-in-one app built for contractors and home-service businesses who need to quote faster, get paid sooner, and run the job without bouncing between five different tools.

Here’s the simple idea. You show up at a job site. You take photos. You jot rough notes. EstimateAce’s AI — powered by Grok — helps turn that into a professional estimate with line items and pricing. Then, in the **same app**, you convert that estimate into an invoice, schedule the work, track labor and mileage, collect a deposit or full payment, and see what you actually made.

No App Store friction required. EstimateAce runs in the browser as a modern web app, and you can install it to your phone’s home screen like a PWA when you want.

If you want one place for estimates, invoices, scheduling, payments, crew, and reports — that’s EstimateAce.

---

### 1) Who it’s for + pricing (≈1 min)

EstimateAce is built for trades and field businesses: general contractors, remodelers, painters, pressure washing, landscaping, handymen, roofing, flooring, cleaning, and similar home-service work.

You get unlimited estimates and invoices. Pricing is straightforward: a **fourteen-day free trial**, then **twenty-nine ninety-nine a month**, or **two hundred forty-nine a year** if you want to save. That includes the AI estimating tools plus the full ops stack — not a stripped “AI-only” plan.

Promotional trials may run longer on special landing pages, but the standard product trial is fourteen days.

---

### 2) Getting started (≈1 min)

Go to estimateace.com or app.estimateace.com. Start your free trial, create your account, and log in.

On first setup, fill in your company profile: business name, contact info, service area, payment preferences, and tax settings. This information shows up on documents your customers see, so take a minute to get it right.

On your phone, open the site in Safari or Chrome and use **Add to Home Screen** if you want a one-tap app icon. You’re ready to build your first estimate from the truck or the kitchen table.

---

### 3) Dashboard tour (≈1 min)

After login you’ll land on your main workspace.

From here you can:

- Start a **New Estimate**
- Open existing estimates and invoices
- Jump into **Schedule** and appointments
- Open **Reports** for paid work and profit
- Manage **Recurring** service plans
- Invite **Crew**
- Adjust **Settings** and billing
- And explore **AI Receptionist** — live phone add-on

Think of the dashboard as mission control. Everything else is one or two taps away.

---

### 4) Building an estimate — the core workflow (≈3.5 min)

Tap **New Estimate**.

Add the customer: name, phone, email, and job address. Address autocomplete helps you move faster and keep records clean.

Then build the scope with line items — descriptions, quantities, and prices. You can type everything yourself, or lean on AI in three powerful ways.

**First: Quote from a job photo.**  
Snap or upload a site photo. AI looks at what it sees and drafts line items and pricing so you’re not staring at a blank page. You always stay in control — edit, delete, or reprice any line before you send it.

**Second: Auto estimate and price.**  
When you know the job type but want consistent materials and labor suggestions, Grok helps fill a structured quote so your pricing stays tighter when you’re busy between jobs.

**Third: Polish the scope.**  
Paste rough notes like “fix house, scrape, prime, two coats, fix fascia.” AI turns that into clear, customer-ready language. You can also translate descriptions when you work with Spanish, French, or other language customers. The app supports English, Spanish, and French built-in, with additional UI language help available.

You can also use **Job Render** ideas: link a site photo to a description line and generate a realistic “after” visualization of completed work — useful for selling the finish, not just the price.

Add tax as needed. You can mark a job tax-exempt, choose whether labor is taxed, and use zip-based tax helpers so totals don’t become a guessing game.

Set a **deposit** percentage if you collect money up front. EstimateAce tracks deposit versus balance due so you’re not juggling sticky notes.

When the document looks right, save it. Your estimate is ready to send — or convert later when the customer says yes.

---

### 5) Field documentation — photos, video, receipts, labor, mileage (≈2 min)

Jobs aren’t just numbers. EstimateAce keeps field proof on the document.

- **Site photos:** capture before, during, and after shots in a photo folder on the estimate or invoice.
- **Video:** record short clips when a still photo isn’t enough.
- **Receipts:** scan or upload material receipts so costs stay attached to the job.
- **Labor:** log hourly labor or a fixed labor amount so your true cost of work is visible.
- **Mileage:** open the mileage tracker to log trips tied to the job — useful for reimbursements and year-end records.

This is how EstimateAce becomes more than a quote tool. It’s a job file: what you promised, what you did, what it cost, and what you collected.

---

### 6) Send to the client, get approval, get paid (≈2.5 min)

When you’re ready, send the estimate to your customer.

Clients can open a clean **approve page** on their phone — review the scope, accept the work, and move toward payment without awkward PDF chains.

You can also share terms links and keep the professional paperwork in the same flow.

When the job is won, hit **Convert to Invoice**. EstimateAce carries the work forward so you’re not rebuilding the same document from scratch.

To get paid, set up your payment methods in settings:

- **Stripe checkout links** for card payments
- **Venmo**
- **Zelle**
- **PayPal**

You can collect a deposit first, then the remaining balance. Mark invoices paid when money lands, and those paid invoices flow into your reports.

There’s also support for client checkout flows tied to the real document totals — so customers pay the amount the system calculated, not a manually mistyped total.

Bottom line: quote → approve → invoice → collect deposit or balance → mark paid. One continuous path.

---

### 7) Schedule, reminders, and recurring services (≈1.5 min)

Open **Schedule** to put jobs on the calendar. Create and edit appointments so your week isn’t living only in your head.

Turn on **daily appointment reminders** if you want morning SMS and email summaries for tomorrow’s work — helpful when crews and customers both need a nudge.

For ongoing customers — lawn plans, monthly washes, maintenance contracts — use **Recurring services**. Create plans, send them to clients, track active and onboarding plans, see when payments are off, and archive canceled plans without losing history.

Recurring is how you turn one-off jobs into steadier monthly revenue inside the same system.

---

### 8) Reports, profit, archives, and export (≈1.5 min)

Under **Reports**, you can see **Paid invoices** and profit views by month so you know what actually came in — not just what you quoted.

Use tax helpers and summaries when you’re preparing books or talking to your accountant.

Need a spreadsheet? **Export selected data to CSV**.

Finished or old documents? **Archive** them so your active lists stay clean. Later, open **View / Retrieve Archives** to reopen or restore documents when a customer calls back about last season’s work.

Paid invoice records and profit reporting stay connected — so deleting or cleaning paid history doesn’t leave your numbers in two different realities.

---

### 9) Crew, settings, languages, and billing (≈1.5 min)

If you have helpers in the field, invite **Crew / sub-contractors** with controlled access to estimates. Crew can work inside the product without getting full owner control of your business settings and billing.

In **Settings**, keep your company profile, payment handles, certificate or license display if you use it, tax defaults, and reminder preferences up to date.

EstimateAce supports multiple languages in the interface so your team can work in English, Spanish, or French, and you can polish or translate customer-facing description text when needed.

Under billing, manage your subscription: trial status, monthly or yearly plan, and the Stripe customer portal when you need to update payment method or cancel.

Account tools also include support contact paths if you need help from the EstimateAce team.

---

### 10) AI Receptionist (live phone add-on) + close (≈1.5 min)

One more feature: **AI Receptionist** — a **live phone** paid add-on.

You keep advertising your existing business number and forward it to a private AI line. When it’s on, AI answers, collects the caller’s name, phone, and address, and logs a lead in your app. When it’s off, calls ring your host number with no announcement. You can also run a test conversation in the app and review the inbox anytime.

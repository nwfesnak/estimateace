**Description** [4000]
EstimateAce is the estimating and invoicing app for contractors and home-service pros. Walk the job, snap a few photos, and send a professional estimate before you leave the driveway.

PHOTO ESTIMATES AND ROOM SCAN
• Snap 1–5 job photos, add a note, and AI drafts priced, editable line items — including upsell suggestions you can accept or ignore
• On LiDAR-equipped iPhones (iPhone 12 Pro or newer Pro models, iOS 16+), scan a room to auto-fill floor and wall area, perimeter, ceiling height, and door and window counts
• On other iPhones, enter room length, width and height manually
• Measurements are a starting point for your estimate and are not guaranteed exact. You review and edit every figure before you send the quote
• Attach before-and-after AI concept renderings, clearly labelled as concept renderings, so customers can see the idea

FAST, ACCURATE ESTIMATES
• Take job-site photos and video right in the app
• AI suggests line items and pricing from your photos and notes, and you stay in control of every number
• Your own prices, materials, labor rates and markups
• Clean, branded PDF estimates with your logo and terms

INVOICES AND GETTING PAID
• Turn an approved estimate into an invoice in one tap
• Send by email or text with a secure link your customer can open on any phone
• Your customers can pay you online by card or bank (powered by Stripe)
• Deposits, progress payments and recurring service plans

STAY ORGANIZED
• Customers, jobs, estimates, invoices and receipts in one place
• Scan receipts and track job costs
• Schedule appointments and send reminders
• Crew logins with permissions, so your team sees only what they should

AI RECEPTIONIST (optional add-on)
• An AI phone line that answers missed calls, takes messages and captures new leads
• Call summaries and transcripts sent to you

Built for roofers, remodelers, painters, landscapers, handymen, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, flooring, fencing, concrete and more.
